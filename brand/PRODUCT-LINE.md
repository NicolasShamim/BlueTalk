# Product line — what Fourthwall can actually make

Checked against Fourthwall's public catalog and branding documentation.

## The two findings

### 1. Half the original range does not exist in print-on-demand

The catalog has **no jackets, no chore coats, no trousers**. Knitwear is limited to
knitted sweaters, V-neck vests and cardigans — there is no quilted vest.

So of the six pieces originally specced, three could not be made:

| Original | Status |
|---|---|
| Work Jacket (canvas chore coat) | **Not available.** No outerwear in the catalog |
| Quilted Vest | **Not available.** Only knitted V-neck vests |
| Utility Trouser | **Not available.** Fleece joggers are the closest thing |

This is not a Fourthwall limitation so much as a print-on-demand one. Cut-and-sew
outerwear needs a real manufacturing run. It is worth noticing that the reference
brand sells tees and hoodies and nothing else — almost certainly for this reason.

### 2. Custom neck labels are not available on on-demand products

Fourthwall's documentation is explicit: *"On-demand products support custom inserts
only. Outer packaging and product labels are handled by the POD fulfillment
network."* Neck labels are customisable **only on bespoke orders**, which carry a
minimum order quantity.

So a garment ordered today arrives with a Comfort Colors tag in the collar. The
"collar marked where the label should be" line has been removed from the site
rather than left on it untrue.

**What replaces it:** custom packaging inserts, $0.25 per order — a 6×4in matte
card in every shipment. That is the one branding surface on-demand gives you, and
it is the right place for the piece's line. A card carrying the line the video ends
on is arguably better than a neck label nobody photographs.

| 6 | Beanie | I remember the sun being closer | €30 |

All six lines survive the swap. The lines were always the product; the garment
underneath them is substitutable, which is the point.

### The hero blank

**Comfort Colors Garment-Dyed Heavyweight T-Shirt** — 100% ring-spun cotton, 6.1oz,
relaxed fit, from about $15.45 with no minimum. Printable front, back and both
sleeves.

This is the closest thing in the catalog to the reference product: garment-dyed,
heavy, boxy, washed rather than flat. Garment dyeing also means **the colour comes
out slightly different every run**, which is a genuine property to build the brand
on rather than a defect to hide.

### Check this before committing

Printed (DTFx) versions of some products are listed as shipping **from the US**,
while embroidered versions ship from US, EU, UK and CA. Fourthwall does run EU
fulfilment and ships to every EU state, but confirm in the dashboard that the
*specific printed products* you pick fulfil from the EU. A Belgian brand shipping
tees from Texas defeats the point, and delivery windows and returns both suffer.

## Tier two, later

The jacket, the vest and real trousers come back through **bespoke** — Fourthwall's
sourcing team, with an MOQ and money up front. That tier also unlocks custom neck
labels, woven labels and hang tags. Worth doing once a line sells; not worth doing
before.

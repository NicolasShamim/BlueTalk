# Video 01 — Long Sleeve

**Line:** *They built me for a colder year*
**Runtime:** ~32s · **Frame:** 4:3 · **Shots:** 8

Every shot is 3–5 seconds because that is where image-to-video holds together.
Generate each one separately from the character sheet plates; never ask for a long
take.

## Shot list

### 1 · 0:00–0:04 — the room
**Image:** Wide. A formal dining room, one long table laid for twelve — cloth,
glasses, folded napkins, all of it immaculate. He sits alone at the head, small in
frame. Warm practical light from above the table, everything beyond it falling to
near-black.
**Motion:** Static. Nothing moves but a faint flicker in the light.
**VO:** *(silence for the first second, then)* "The heating in this building runs on
a schedule from 1974."
**Caption:** `The heating in this building runs on a schedule from 1974`
**Sound:** Room tone. A low boiler hum underneath, and keep it there all video.

> The first second has no words. The empty twelve settings do the work — that is the
> entire premise delivered before he speaks.

**Motion:** He breathes. A slow blink. Nothing else.

## Rules for this one

- **Grade every shot the same.** Crushed blacks, warm key against cold shadow, fine
  grain over everything. Build it once as a preset in the edit and never touch it.
- **Captions:** large, white, heavy, thin black outline, centred low. TikTok default.
  Do not make them tasteful — that is a different channel.
- **The cut to shot 8 is hard.** No fade, no transition, no music sting. The jump
  from render to photograph is the point.
- **No music.** Room tone, fabric, and silence. If it feels empty, it is working.
- Label as AI-generated on both platforms.

## Caption and tags for the post

> He has not been told.

`#soldanyway` and nothing else. The on-screen text carries it; the written caption
exists only to give people something to reply to.

## Production order

1. Character sheet — six plates, locked seed *(needs image credits)*
2. Shots 1–7 as stills from those plates, then image-to-video, 3–5s each
3. Shot 8 from the real garment photograph
4. Assemble, grade, caption
5. Sound last, and give it real time — it is half the result

What is collected

One thing: the email address you type into the waitlist form, and the date you submitted it. Nothing else. There is no account, no profile, no tracking pixel, and no advertising network on this site.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which — like any web host — records requests to its servers, including IP addresses, for security and operational reasons. That happens whether or not you submit anything.

Why, and for how long

Your address is used for exactly one thing: to email you when the first pieces go on sale. That is the purpose you consented to by submitting it, and it is the only purpose it will be used for.

It is kept until you unsubscribe or ask for it to be deleted, and in any case no longer than is needed for that purpose. It is never sold, rented, or shared for anyone else's marketing.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time — every email carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes the address.

Write to [email address] and it will be dealt with within a month. If you are unhappy with how that goes, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels.

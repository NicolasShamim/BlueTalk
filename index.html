01

The mark

Inside back neck. Oxide red. Nothing on the outside.

Every garment carries the same detail: four stitches where the Bureau tag was cut out.

It is not a logo. It is the wound left behind when a garment stops being state property and starts being yours. You will not see it on the street. Whoever is wearing one knows it is there, and so do you.

Room 12

Two floors under a laundry there is a bench, one warm lamp, and a man who has been doing this long enough to be bored of the risk. He has no politics. He thinks badly-made things are an insult, and he fixes them.

Everything here was issued to somebody. Reinforced, re-dyed, re-cut, and returned in a condition the Bureau would describe as defaced.

The detail

Four red stitches at the inside back neck.

No logo on the outside of any piece. The only marking is on the inside of the collar, where a label would normally go. You know it's there. Nobody else has to.

The pieces

Six, and only six. A small range means every piece gets sampled properly before it goes on sale, and it's what the character actually wears in the videos.

How it works

  1. You order

    Pick a piece and a size. You pay once, at checkout, with VAT already included in the price you see. No customs surprises inside the EU.

  2. It gets made

    Your piece is printed and finished after you order it, by a production partner in the EU. Nothing sits in a warehouse, so nothing gets dumped at the end of a season.

  3. It ships to you

    Straight from the EU facility, usually within a week. Tracked. If something arrives wrong, it gets replaced — you don't pay twice.

The channel

Short videos, one recurring character, and a joke you can wear.

Every video follows the same shape: he talks, it gets progressively more unhinged, and the last line he says is the line printed across the piece he's wearing. That's the whole format. The clothes aren't advertised in the videos — they're the punchline of them.

Posted to TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Nothing is filmed; the character is rendered, which is why he looks identical in every single video.
